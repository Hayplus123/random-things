Power-Ups

⚡ Speed Boost

Increases running speed by +60% for 7 seconds.

🛡 Energy Shield

Blocks 1 tag attempt and deflects the chaser.

🦘 Super Jump

Increases jump power by +75% to leap across tiers.

🔬 Shrink

Halves your size to dodge incoming tags and squeeze through gaps.
